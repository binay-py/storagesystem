set up your chest

four steps. the only fiddly one is finding your channel id, so this page does that part for you.

checking this deployment...
1

make a bot

message @BotFather on telegram, send /newbot, and follow the prompts. it hands you a token that looks like 8123456:AAH...

give that token to your worker:

wrangler secret put TG_BOT_TOKEN

the token is a secret, not a var. it never goes in wrangler.toml and never touches git.

2

make a private channel and post once

in telegram: new channel, set it to private, then add your bot as an admin with permission to post.

now send any message in that channel. a single "hi" is enough. the bot has to see one message before telegram will tell anyone the channel exists.

3

find the channel id

press the button. this reads your bot's recent updates and lists every channel it can see.

4

save it and deploy

put the id in wrangler.toml, replacing the SETUP placeholder:

[vars]
TG_CHAT_ID = "-1001234567890"

then:

wrangler deploy

this page and its endpoints switch off automatically once TG_CHAT_ID holds a real id. nothing to remember to disable.

keep the bot token and your chest key in two places you control. if the channel is deleted or the bot is removed from it, the files are gone, and nobody can recover them for you. that is the trade you accepted when you chose a chest only you can open.
